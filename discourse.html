Who is saying what about PJM and the power market

Podcasts, regulator webcasts and conference stages hold much of the real argument about the grid: who pays for data centers, whether the capacity market works, what regulators will do next. This page maps that landscape, pulls out the best lines, profiles the voices worth following, and then does the part most commentary skips: it checks whether what people predicted came true.

How to read this. The catalogue is a curated sample, not a census. Quotes are short (under 15 words), checked against the published transcript, and linked to the episode; full transcripts stay private. Everything else is paraphrase. The prediction scoreboard has very few resolved claims, so it shows who is checkable, not who is good. Research and education, not investment advice.
