Natural gas: what it costs, what it does to PJM power, and a new way to trade it

Gas sets the price of PJM power at the margin, so it sits behind most of what we study. This page puts the gas price, the gas-to-power margin and the new 24/7 Henry Hub perpetual (trade[XYZ] on Hyperliquid, with ICE planning a regulated US version) in one place. Everything here is research on paper: we do not trade, and none of it is advice.

What we could test, on paper

1 · How tightly does the perp follow the futures?Needs front-month CME prices to compare against; we have only spot. The spot comparison above says the daily moves differ a lot, which is expected, but it is not the right test.
2 · Is funding a crowding signal?The rate swings from deeply negative to extremely positive. Does an extreme reading precede a gas move over the next few days? Needs more history than the 9 months that exist.
3 · Gas against powerSpark spreads are at records with gas flat, so power drives them. A gas leg paired with a power-linked stock would isolate that. The four generators we follow (CEG, VST, TLN, NRG) are on trade[XYZ]; GE Vernova (GEV) is.

None of these is registered or running. They are the order we would do them in, and each would be written down and sealed in the forward log before any data arrives, like H13 to H15. Paper only, never advice.
