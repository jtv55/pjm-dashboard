Peg Stress: is PJM's price ceiling about to be tested?

Some of the biggest market moves came when a price held in place by policy met a force it could not hold back. PJM's capacity price now sits at a policy ceiling while demand grows faster than supply can be built. This page lists what would show that strain is real, and what would show it is not, each with a date and a way to be wrong.

Read this first. This is a watchlist and a record of dated forecasts, not advice and not a trade idea. Probabilities are our judgment and are scored later. Nothing here is a position. Any paper position would run through the forward ledger, and real money is the owner's decision. Facts are from the linked sources as of Nov 8, 2026; items marked "unverified" were not confirmed.

1. Why the analogy, and where it breaks

What was heldWhat pushed on itHow it resolved
UK pound, 1992A fixed exchange-rate bandHigh German rates; a weak UK economyGovernment could not afford the rates needed to defend it, and left the band
Aluminum, early 1990sA walled-off Soviet supplyRegime collapseStockpiles reached world markets and prices fell
Texas Railroad Commission to OPEC, 1970sSpare capacity that capped the world priceOutput peaked and spare capacity ran outPricing power moved to producers abroad
PJM capacity, nowAn auction price collar set by regulatorsLoad growth, retirements, slow new buildUnresolved. Open: collar relaxed, bills fall on households, supply arrives, or demand slips

Where it breaks. Those moves paid off because the market believed the peg would hold. Here the market already doubts it: data-center load and capacity prices are widely covered, and the generator stocks have already moved a lot. So "nobody sees it" is the weakest part of the idea. What may still be mispriced is the timing and form of the release, not the existence of the strain.

4. The two new scored calls

  • New winter record, 20%. PJM's hourly load exceeds 143,700 MW at least once between Dec 1, 2026 and Feb 28, 2027. Low because Fern's forecasts of up to 148 GW did not materialize and the peak came in near 139 GW. Wrong if no hour exceeds it.
  • Turbine backlog, 85%. GE Vernova reports gas equipment backlog plus slot reservations of at least 130 GW with its Q4 2026 results, published by Feb 28, 2027. Management guided to this level. Wrong if the figure is lower or not reported on that basis.

5. What this does not do

  • It cannot be backtested. A policy-peg release is a one-time event, so the overnight search has nothing to test. The only honest test is dated calls scored as they resolve.
  • It gives no position sizes or entries. Whether any indicator should become a paper position is a separate decision.
  • It may be priced in. If the generator stocks already discount a break, being right about the strain pays nothing. That is the main reason to keep these as scored calls first.
