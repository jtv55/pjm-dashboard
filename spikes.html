How PJM counts each resource as capacity

Energy is megawatt-hours over the whole year. Capacity is megawatts you can count on in the worst hours, which is what PJM's auction buys. A battery makes no energy: it moves cheap hours' energy into the peak. It is counted at about 57% because long events can drain it. Data-center load is flat all day, so it lifts the peak too unless it can be curtailed, which is why the connect-and-manage rules matter.
