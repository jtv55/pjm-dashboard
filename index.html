Can TabPFN forecast the PJM real-time minus day-ahead spread?

Spread is the real-time LMP minus the day-ahead LMP, in $/MWh. Positive means real-time cleared above day-ahead. Each hour below was forecast by a model trained only on earlier data.

How this was built

  • Model: TabPFN v3.5 regressor via the TabPFN API, retrained every 3 days on the prior 75 days.
  • Features known the day before: hour, weekday, DA price and congestion, DA price versus its 7-day average, and spread history lagged 48 hours.
  • Data: PJM Data Miner 2, verified hourly day-ahead and real-time LMPs at Western Hub.
  • Not included: fees, virtual bid uplift, position limits, or any fundamentals such as load or wind forecasts.
