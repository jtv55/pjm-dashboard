Intelligence · from a fuel to a token to a dollar

How much intelligence is in a cubic foot of gas?

The chain from burning or harnessing a fuel to a model producing a word: heat to electricity to chips to FLOPs to tokens to dollars, with every constant sourced or flagged as an assumption, and then PJM's data-center load run through it. Change any assumption below and everything on the page recomputes.

Treat the outputs as orders of magnitude. The conversion constants for fuels are solid. The chip and model constants move every year, real fleets mix hardware and models, and "a token" is not one fixed unit. The defaults are one reasonable setup, not a measurement of any company.
