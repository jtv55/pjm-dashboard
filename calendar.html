How to read this

Impact runs from -2 (hurts) to +2 (helps). "Gen" is merchant generators, "load" is data centers and other large customers. Where a stock basket exists (generators, utilities, gas, equipment), the scorecard checks the sign of the score against the basket's abnormal return over the event day and the next day. Annotations were written after the fact, so a match is consistency, not skill. Events with only a month known are listed but not used in return calculations. Open dockets and comment deadlines are added as they are verified.
