What has earned trust, and what has not

This project writes down every idea and its test before running it, records the nulls, and refuses to trade anything that has not survived a forward test. This page is the whole record in one place: how ideas move from a hunch to something worth money, what the evidence for each looks like, what is being tested live, and what would have to be true before real capital goes anywhere near it.

Read this first. Research and education, not investment advice. No real-money trades have been made. Every performance number is gross of costs unless it says otherwise, and every in-sample result is a lead, not a result.

Why we did not chase Talen's +7.3%

Talen reported on Nov 3 and rose from $332.99 to $357.28. The company narrowed 2026 guidance around the same midpoint, gave 2027 ranges of about 26% to 27% growth at the midpoints, and upsized its buyback. Its earlier guidance raise was already in the price.

We tested the obvious ways to be "all over it". Earnings straddles (H12) lost about 6% gross against random days. And across 41 earnings reactions at VST, CEG, TLN and NRG since March 2024, the stock then lagged SPY by 2.0% (standard error 1.0%) over the next five sessions; after reactions above +3% it lagged by 2.7% (10 events). There is no sign that buying a strong print pays. What we registered instead is the opposite bet, H16, as a paper test, with TLN as its first event. It is a lead found on the same data, about two standard errors, so it earns nothing until the forward sample says so.
