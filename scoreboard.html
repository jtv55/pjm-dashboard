What has earned trust, and what has not

This project writes down every idea and its test before running it, records the nulls, and refuses to trade anything that has not survived a forward test. This page is the whole record in one place: how ideas move from a hunch to something worth money, what the evidence for each looks like, what is being tested live, and what would have to be true before real capital goes anywhere near it.

Read this first. Research and education, not investment advice. No real-money trades have been made. Every performance number is gross of costs unless it says otherwise, and every in-sample result is a lead, not a result.
