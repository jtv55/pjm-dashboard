Residential · twelve monthly electric bills, one house ·

What one house's electric bills say about the grid

Twelve statements, October 2025 to October 2026, from a regulated utility that buys power for customers who don't shop for a supplier. Everything below is computed from the bills: where each dollar goes, what the weather does to the meter, what a kilowatt-hour really costs at the margin, and how the wholesale market we study shows up, late and smoothed, on a household bill.

One household, shared with permission. This is the owner's own data, published on purpose. The statements' names, addresses, account, customer and meter numbers were never read into the dataset and appear nowhere on this page. Twelve bills from one house are an anecdote, not a sample: read every pattern as "this is what it looked like here".
